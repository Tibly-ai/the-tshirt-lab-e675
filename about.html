OUR THREE COMMANDMENTS

No shortcuts, no compromises, and zero generic blanks. Here is how we print.

/ / Standards & Practices
01/

WATER-BASED DISCHARGE INKS

We primarily print with premium water-based and discharge inks. Unlike heavy plastisol that feels like a thick plastic shield on your chest, discharge inks dye the fabric fibers directly. You get a soft print that breathes and ages beautifully.

Eco-Responsibility 100% Water-Soluble
02/

HAND-PULLED IN AUSTIN, TX

Every shirt is loaded, pulled, and verified on our manual presses by actual printmakers. This slow-batch process guarantees razor-sharp registration and proper ink deposit depth that fully automatic industrial carousels simply overlook.

Human Touch Manual Press Rig
03/

HEAVY-DUTY CUSTOM BLANKS

We partner with mills that supply heavy-weight cotton blanks ranging from 6.5 oz to 8.2 oz. They hold crisp structures, resist shrinking, and feature double-needle stitched collars that maintain their tight fit wash after wash.
