LAB STANDARDS

THE SPECIFICATIONS
THAT SEPARATE US
FROM THE APPS.

Automated print-on-demand sites operate on margin and speed. We operate on mechanics, pressure, and ink chemistry. We burn our own screens, mix custom pigments by hand, and cure each run manually.

50 piece minimum batch run.

To maintain high manual quality control, we do not print single runs.

100% Vector Pre-Flight

Every artwork submitted undergoes separation. We rebuild low-resolution layers to ensure razor-sharp edges on the screen mesh.

Custom Color Matching

We mix ink using physical color-matching catalogs. Tell us your target Pantone, and we match it under laboratory daylight conditions.

Premium Sizing Matrix

From XS to 5XL, we balance print dimensions across garment size batches so your graphic looks scale-correct on every body.

Tunnel-Dryer Cured

No heat press shortcuts. All shirts run through a high-temperature infrared tunnel belt to solidify the plastisol matrix permanently.
